## Why AGPLv3

MEM is network-based, self-hosted software intended to run infrastructure that operators control.

AGPLv3 was chosen because it is a strong open-source license designed to preserve source availability when modified software is distributed or used to provide network services, subject to the license's terms.

For MEM, the practical goals are to support:

- operator trust and inspectability;
- the ability to study and modify the software;
- meaningful source availability for network-deployed modifications; and
- a self-hosting model that does not depend on closed-source control-plane software.

## What AGPLv3 does not do

- prevent forks;

- prevent competing services;
- grant trademark or branding rights;
- define project governance; or
- define support, hosting, consulting, or enterprise offerings.

Those subjects are separate from the software license.
